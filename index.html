Studio Policies

Deposit

A $15 non-refundable deposit goes toward your service and is required to book all appointments.

Late Arrival

Please arrive on time. There is a 10 minute grace period. Arriving late may reduce appointment time and may result in an incomplete look.

Refunds

Strictly no refunds. Complimentary touch ups are offered for any issues within 72 hours.

Guests

You are allowed to bring 1 guest. Extra seating space is limited in the home studio.

No Foreign Fills

Please book a removal and full set if you have lashes done by another lash tech.

Makeup

Please do not wear eye makeup before your appointment.

Infill Note

If you have less than 40% of your lashes remaining, it will be considered a full set. Please book accordingly.

Other Notes

Address is sent after booking. Please arrive 5 minutes early for your first appointment to fill out the consent form.
