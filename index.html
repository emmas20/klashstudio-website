The easiest way to book is to DM me on Instagram @NailsByXia or fill out the booking form on this page. I'll confirm your appointment within 24 hours and send a deposit link to secure your spot.

A gel manicure takes about 60–75 minutes. Acrylic sets typically take 90–120 minutes. Appointments with nail art may take longer depending on the design complexity. I'll always give you a realistic time estimate when you book.

Yes! I love doing bridal parties, birthdays, and girls' nights. Group bookings for 3+ people receive priority scheduling and a small group discount. Reach out directly to discuss availability and packages.

Come with clean, polish-free nails if possible. Avoid moisturizing your hands/feet the morning of. For gel or acrylic services, skip hand lotion before arriving. And bring inspo photos — the more the better!

I use professional-grade, cruelty-free products. If you have known allergies or sensitivities, please let me know when booking so I can recommend the most suitable service and products for you.
