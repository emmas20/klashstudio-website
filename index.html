Before your appointment

Frequently asked.

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Please arrive with clean lashes and no mascara, eyeliner, strip-lash glue or oil-based products around the eye area.

How long will my appointment take?

Appointment length depends on the service selected. Square shows the estimated duration before you confirm your booking.

How often should I book a fill?

Most clients return every two to three weeks depending on natural lash growth, aftercare and desired fullness.

Where can I view booking policies?

Please review the policies shown during your Square booking process before confirming your appointment.
